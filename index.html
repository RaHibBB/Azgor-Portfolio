The page where they decide to buy.

A weak product page wastes your ad budget. I design product pages that build trust, work on mobile first, and turn visitors into customers.

Anatomy of a product page

  1. A

    Above the fold. Image and video gallery with zoom, bold price with compare-at discount, color swatches, and a sticky add-to-cart bar on mobile.

  2. B

    Trust and social proof. Loox, Judge.me or Okendo reviews with photos, payment and secure-checkout badges, free shipping and guarantee badges.

  3. C

    Content blocks. Collapsible tabs for specs, shipping and FAQs, comparison tables, benefit icons, and customer or promo video.

  4. D

    Upsells. Frequently bought together bundles and “Buy 2, get 10% off” quantity breaks to raise your average order value.

  5. E

    Mobile and speed. Buttons, images and text laid out for thumbs, with lazy-loaded images and lean scripts for fast loading.

Orders that ship themselves.

Fast, accurate fulfillment keeps customers happy, cuts refund requests and keeps your payment gateways healthy. I connect your store, your suppliers and your customers.

Tracking · one order’s journey

  1. Order placedA customer checks out on your store.
  2. Sent to supplierThe order forwards automatically through DSers, AutoDS, CJdropshipping, Zendrop, Spocket or AliExpress.
  3. ShippedThe tracking number syncs back to Shopify as soon as it ships.
  4. Customer updatedAutomatic email and SMS updates, plus a tracking page in your menu with 17TRACK, ParcelPanel or AfterShip.
  5. DeliveredAnd if something goes wrong, I handle it: see below.
