# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, hosted on Vercel (user's choice).

## Users

Shopify store owners, mostly dropshippers, from someone about to launch a first store to an owner who has too much daily work. They land on the site from a marketplace profile, social post or referral. They are deciding whether to hire this freelancer for a specific job (research, listing, page design, fulfillment) or as a full-time VA.

## Product Purpose

Personal services site for Azgor Hossin, a freelance Shopify dropshipping and e-commerce specialist. It explains what he does and moves a visitor to contact him on WhatsApp or email, or to hire him through Fiverr or Upwork.

## Positioning

One person who covers the whole dropshipping store lifecycle: finding the product, listing it, designing the page it sells on, automating fulfillment, and then running the store day to day as a VA. He also builds WooCommerce funnels with Elementor + CartFlows/FunnelKit.

## Operating Context

Tools named in his service copy: AdSpy, Dropispy, BigSpy, Drop Point, Ecomhunt, AdHunter, AliShark (research); DSers, AutoDS, Zendrop, Spocket, CJdropshipping (fulfillment); Loox, Judge.me, Okendo (reviews); 17TRACK, ParcelPanel, AfterShip (tracking); Elementor, CartFlows, FunnelKit (WooCommerce).
Product research deliverable is a spreadsheet: product name, AliExpress link/price/orders/rating, profit margin and markup, FB ad link, competitor link/price, Google Trends, monthly search volume.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services: store setup, product research (current winning / untapped / in trend / upcoming trend), product listing and SEO, product page design, theme customization, order fulfillment automation, full-time Shopify VA, Elementor + CartFlows/FunnelKit funnels, logo and branding assets.
Contact: WhatsApp, email, Fiverr and Upwork. Real links and numbers are not provided yet and live as placeholders in one config block.

## Brand Commitments

## Evidence on Hand

Only the claims in the source copy: worked on 200+ stores, over the last 2+ years. No testimonials, client names, logos, screenshots, ratings or revenue figures. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work product (the research sheet, the page anatomy), not adjectives.
2. Every section ends in a way to contact him.
3. Plain, direct English; many clients and the owner are non-native speakers.
4. Honest proof only; leave marked slots rather than invented reviews.
